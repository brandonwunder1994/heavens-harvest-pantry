# Launch verification — October 8, 2026

Production: https://heavensharvestpantry.com

Released deployment: https://heavens-harvest-pantry-pn25cloeo-brandon-wunder.vercel.app

## Result

The email configuration no longer blocks checkout. The live Stripe payment form loaded on desktop and mobile with the expected $10.16 total ($10.00 food plus $0.16 tax). The final Pay control was reachable on mobile with no horizontal overflow. No payment was submitted and no customer email was sent. Both marked unpaid verification sessions were expired and their reservations released.

Verified paid orders retain their on-screen receipt when email is unavailable, email delivery throws, or refreshing the email status fails. The receipt includes purchased items, tax and total, pickup date and period, storage guidance, private pickup details and gift delivery details as applicable. Unpaid or unverified sessions cannot reveal the private address or clear the basket. Failed email status remains available for owner retry.

- Set the production origin and Stripe return links to the custom domain. Added the www-to-apex redirect, canonical metadata, sitemap and robots exclusions.
- Updated customer, print and label links. Updated the saved label QR origin without replacing other saved label content. Regenerated and decoded both SVG QR assets, preserving the sage corner artwork and gold heart.
- Preserved the existing Stripe webhook endpoint and verified its paid and expiry subscriptions.
- Corrected the sound switch ARIA warning and launch documentation.
- Made the normal production build compile-only, removing the unrelated mock database migration from the build command. Added an explicitly enabled protected-build readiness check.

## Limits and follow-up

- Email service configuration remains absent by the approved launch default. Customers are told to save the complete on-screen receipt. Enabling confirmation email later requires Resend configuration; no real delivery was tested.
- Stripe test-mode credentials were unavailable. Actual Stripe sandbox card success and decline payments were **not** completed. Decline/retry and paid-receipt paths passed isolated simulation and signed-event integration tests; these are not a substitute for an actual sandbox card payment.
- Owner browser checks used isolated sample access. Production owner passwords and customer orders were not changed. Authentication and persistence are also tested with disposable credentials/storage.
- Print layouts and PNG export were verified in the browser; physical printer output was not tested.
- These checks establish the tested launch behavior, not a guarantee against every possible defect or a formal WCAG conformance certification.
