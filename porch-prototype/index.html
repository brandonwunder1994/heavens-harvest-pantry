The rule that matters

A light she forgets goes out on its own

She bakes weekly, so three weeks of silence means the switch is not being flipped — not that nothing has changed. After twenty-one days the lamp disappears rather than telling people she is baking on the strength of a switch nobody has touched since April. Her own page warns her five days before it happens, so it is never a surprise.
