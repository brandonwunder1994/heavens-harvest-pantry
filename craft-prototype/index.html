Phase three · four ideas

The things people feel and cannot name

Nothing new appears. Everything already there stops looking cheap in the four ways people notice without knowing why.

One of four

Type that behaves

Invisible when it is right and cheap-looking the moment it is wrong. Four things: nothing jumps as the fonts load, headlines break where a person would break them, prices line up in a column, and quotation marks hang outside the text edge so the margin stays straight.

Two of four

Real paper

Cotton stock, pressed. The fibre catches light unevenly, the press bit a few millimetres in so the edge sits proud, and the ink sank a hair into the surface instead of sitting on it. All three are drawn in code, so there is nothing to wait for and nothing to license.

Three of four

The oven timer

Instead of the spinner that belongs to every app on earth. Two seconds of waiting becomes two seconds of her brand. It winds down at a real rate rather than spinning forever pretending to know how long is left.

Four of four

One sound, once

A soft crunch when something goes into the basket. Nothing else on the site makes a sound, and it is off until you switch it on. The crunch is built in the browser — a short burst of filtered noise — so there is no audio file to fetch and no licence to buy.
