Worth stealing from
The official three.js example gallery is open source and free to build on, and it already contains working versions of the pour, the material morph and the scroll-driven camera. Other demo archives are worth studying but set their own terms, so we read those before borrowing. Either way we are not starting from a blank page on any of the hard parts.
One thing to avoid
Spline and the other drag-and-drop 3D tools are quick to demo and then charge monthly, ship a heavy player, and own the file. For a scene this specific, hand-built is lighter, cheaper and hers permanently. I would not use one here.
