No pressure, no pitch

Want to know Jesus?

Then this page is the whole of it. We are a family that bakes granola in a home kitchen, and we follow Jesus. If you have questions, or you are looking for a church, or you just want somebody to sit next to on a Sunday, there is a seat for you and we will meet you at the door.
It is not a performance
You do not need to clean yourself up first, know the songs, or have the answers. Nobody in the room has them either. You are welcome exactly as you are today.
It is being there for people
Most of what being a Christian looks like day to day is showing up: meals when somebody is sick, a hand when somebody moves, sitting with people in the hard weeks. That is the job.
And telling the good news
The other half is saying out loud why we do it. Jesus came, died and rose so that anybody who wants to come home can. That is the news, and it is meant to be shared, not kept.
Where our family goes
Pure Heart Church
It is our home church here in the north valley, and it is where our family is on a Sunday. We are not asking you to leave anywhere. If you already have a church, good. If you do not, or you are between churches, or you have not set foot in one in twenty years, come and sit with us once and see.
