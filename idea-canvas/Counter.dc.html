Idea · Walk her kitchen

The whole page is one room. Scrolling is the camera.

No sections, no bands, no "About Us". Five stops on one slow move through the morning she bakes, with the words arriving where they belong. The scroll bar becomes a timeline of how it is made.
Stop one
The bowl
Oats, honey, oil, salt. The camera looks straight down as it is folded by hand. Ingredients name themselves as they land.
Stop two
The oven
The glass goes warm and the whole page's light turns golden for a moment. Twenty-two minutes, stirred twice.
Stop three
The cooling
Steam rises and the clusters set. This is the stop where the flavors appear and you pick yours.
Stop four
The jar
It fills, the lid seals, the label goes on with the batch date. This is where the price and the button live.
Stop five
The porch
Camera pulls back to a doorstep with the bag on it and the porch light on. The closing blessing sits here.
"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart."
Colossians 3:23
If this proves too heavy for phones, the same five stops work as five still frames that cross-fade. The story survives; only the camera move is lost.
