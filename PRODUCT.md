# Heaven's Harvest Pantry

## Platform
web

## Stack
Existing Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Neon Postgres and Stripe.

## Users
Lauren operates a small pantry from her phone and computer. Customers buy granola and candied almonds for porch pickup or send anonymous blessing gifts.

## Product Purpose
The owner portal makes business activity, next actions, prices and fulfillment easy to understand without opening every editor or order.

## Operating Context
Tuesday 9 p.m. America/Phoenix cutoff. Friday/Saturday pickup share a 48 lb bake. Chocolate needs porch refrigeration. Blessing gifts deliver Friday in Vistancia. Printing uses the existing T50M Pro label exports and letter-size cards.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Six owner destinations: Dashboard, Orders, Bake & pickup, Products & prices, Print studio, Settings. Quick size-price editing plus one detailed product editor at a time. Preserve authentication, historical snapshots, old URLs, reward balances, tax configuration and existing workspace changes. No automatic deployment or historical-data deletion. Scheduling, capacity and payment rules remain fixed. Practice orders never enter live operations.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the existing logo and cream, brown and sage palette. Business controls use readable sans-serif text, consistent actions and plain instructions.

## Product Principles
- Summarize first; disclose details when needed.
- One authoritative current price per product size.
- Unavailable data is never a zero or a successful save.
- Preserve purchased facts independently of current menu edits.
- Keep retired features out of everyday work.
